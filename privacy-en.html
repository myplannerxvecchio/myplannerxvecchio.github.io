Who we are and contact

My Planner is an app for organizing tasks, schedules, notes and habits. This policy explains how we handle information when you use the app. The responsible brand is My Planner. Contact myplannerxvecchio@gmail.com for privacy questions or support.

Local mode and optional account

You can use the planner without creating an account. In local mode, content stays on the device and is not synced with Supabase. If you create an account and sign in, that account’s content is also stored in the cloud to sync across your devices. The local planner remains separate from each account’s planner.

Information we handle

With an account, we handle your email address, a user identifier and the information you choose to save: priorities, tasks, schedules, written reminders, notes and manually entered water and exercise records. Your password is sent to the authentication service to manage access; it is not included in your notes or exported planner backups. The infrastructure provider may retain IP addresses, authentication events and technical logs associated with requests.

How we use information

We use information to create and protect your account, confirm your email, recover access, save and sync your planner and respond to inquiries you send us. Technical logs help keep the service secure and working. We do not sell your planner content or use it for advertising or tracking across apps.

Providers and storage

We use Supabase for authentication and the database. The app’s project is hosted in Oregon, United States; using sync means sending information to that infrastructure. Account emails are handled through Supabase and its configured email service. They use the recipient’s address and information needed to confirm or recover access, not task or note content. These public pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process IP addresses and technical visit logs for operation and security.

Water, exercise and permissions

You enter water and exercise records manually. The app does not read HealthKit or health sensors. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts or location. You can allow local notifications and disable them in system settings. Schedule notifications do not display the text of your notes.

Apple Watch and backups

Apple Watch exchanges information with the paired iPhone and keeps a cache of days already received. The iPhone may sync changes with your account. Exporting a backup creates a file containing planner content; you choose where to save it and whom to share it with. Operating system backups are also governed by your device settings.

Security

The app connects to Supabase over HTTPS. Session credentials are stored in the system Keychain, and access to cloud data is separated by account. Local files stay in the app’s storage. Protect access to your devices and exported backups. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and deletion

Planner content is retained while you keep the account or local copies. In Settings → Delete my account, you can request permanent deletion of your authentication user and its planner data from the active database. On completion, the app signs out and removes that account’s copy from the requesting device. Signing out alone does not delete the account or its data. Exported files and offline copies on other devices must be deleted separately. Technical logs and any provider backups may follow their own retention cycles and applicable obligations; we do not promise their immediate deletion.

Your choices and requests

You can edit your planner, export a copy and delete your account in the app. For questions or requests about access, correction, deletion or other applicable rights, contact myplannerxvecchio@gmail.com. We may request reasonable information to verify account ownership. We will never ask for your password by email or ask you to send us a recovery link.

Policy changes

We will update this policy when features, providers or data handling change. The version date appears at the beginning. If a change requires additional notice or authorization, we will provide it before that processing begins.
