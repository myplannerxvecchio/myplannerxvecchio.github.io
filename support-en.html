Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

You can use the planner locally. To sync between iPhone, iPad and Mac, sign in with the same account on each device.

How do I reset my password?

In Settings, select Forgot my password. Request the email and open its link on a device with My Planner installed. Choose and confirm your new password. Request a new link if it has expired. Do not share the link or your password.

How do I delete my account?

In Settings → Delete my account, review the warning and confirm only if you want to permanently delete your account and its cloud planner. You can export a backup first. Offline copies on other devices and exported files must be deleted separately.

Why is a change missing on another device?

Check your connection and that both devices use the same account. Open Settings → Sync now. If changes need review, choose the version to keep. Offline changes remain saved on the device.

How does Apple Watch work?

The watch connects through the paired iPhone. Open My Planner on the iPhone and tap Refresh on the watch. You can browse other days; dates not yet saved on the watch need an iPhone connection. Tasks and habits can be changed for the current day.

How do I enable reminders?

In Settings, enable the daily reminder or schedule activity notifications. Allow notifications in system settings. Each device is configured separately. The Remember section stores text and does not schedule a notification on its own.
